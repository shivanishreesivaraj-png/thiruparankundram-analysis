# thiruparankundram-analysis
A multidisciplinary historical, archaeological, statistical, and legal research project exploring Thiruparankundram Hill, Madurai, through historical literature, Jain inscriptions, religious heritage, land records, judicial decisions, and constitutional rights using Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.
# Thiruparankundram Hill: Historical, Archaeological & Legal Data Analysis

## Project Overview

This project presents a multidisciplinary study of **Thiruparankundram Hill, Madurai, Tamil Nadu**, examining its historical significance, archaeological heritage, religious traditions, land records, judicial proceedings, and constitutional dimensions through data collection, statistical analysis, and visualization.

The project aims to organize historical and legal evidence into a structured dataset and analyse it using Python-based data science techniques.

## Research Areas

- **Historical Heritage:** Sangam literature, historical references, and religious traditions.
- **Archaeological Heritage:** Rock-cut structures, inscriptions, and architectural evidence.
- **Religious Heritage:** Subramaniya Swamy Temple, Sikandar Badusha Dargah, and Jain heritage.
- **Legal Research:** Historical judgments, court proceedings, and judicial findings.
- **Land Records:** Property documentation, ownership, possession, and administrative records.
- **Constitutional Rights:** Relevant constitutional provisions and judicial interpretations.
- **Statistical Analysis:** Evidence distribution, source verification, chronology, and category comparisons.

## Project Objectives

1. Build a structured research database for Thiruparankundram Hill.
2. Organize historical, archaeological, legal, and administrative records.
3. Analyse the distribution and verification status of collected evidence.
4. Visualize historical and documentary information.
5. Identify research gaps and areas requiring further verification.
6. Present findings through a transparent, data-driven research approach.

## Research Methodology

The project distinguishes between historical evidence, archaeological dating, legal ownership, possession, religious usage, and administrative control.

Historical antiquity alone is not treated as proof of land ownership or exclusive religious rights. Sources are evaluated according to their type, reliability, and verification status.

Statistical analysis is used to describe the collected evidence, not to predict court judgments or determine legal entitlements.
